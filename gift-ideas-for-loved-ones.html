Gift Ideas to Give Your Loved Ones

Thoughtful experiences, cozy favorites, and useful little finds for the people you love.

1. Concert Tickets

There’s nothing quite like getting to see your favorite band perform in person! Concert tickets are a great gift for someone who prefers experiences over material things. They give them something exciting to look forward to and a memory they can cherish for years.

2. A Book You’ve Been Wanting to Read

You know that one book you’ve been dying to read but just can’t get around to because checking it out from the library and having a deadline feels overwhelming? By owning the book, you can read it whenever you finally have the time.

3. Glamnetic Press-On Nails

My nails have always been flimsy and weak. After trying supplements and strengthening polishes, nothing seemed to work long-term. I started wearing press-ons in high school and loved finally having strong, cute nails, but I hated how quickly they popped off.

After trying several brands, a pillar of light seemed to shine down upon Glamnetic nails. At first, I was hesitant because of the price, but I soon found that they were worth it. These nails are durable, easy to apply, and usually stay on two to three days longer than other press-ons I’ve tried. They also come in many cute designs and are reusable.

Pros

  • Stay on longer than other press-ons I’ve tried.
  • Durable.
  • Quick and easy to apply.
  • Available in many cute designs.
  • Reusable.

Drawbacks

  • Like any press-on nails, they can still occasionally pop off.
  • If glue residue isn’t cleaned off properly, it can reduce how long the nail lasts when you reuse it.

4. AirTag

If you’re anything like me and lose things often, an AirTag can be a game changer! I lose things A LOT, and having an AirTag makes life so much easier.

I like using mine on my car keys and even in my car, which is especially helpful when I’m parked in a massive parking lot. I also have one on my water bottle. It isn’t necessary, but it makes life much easier since I lose it so much, haha.

If you have a pet, you can attach one to their collar. If you travel often, you can put one inside your luggage. You could even use one on your TV remote if you’re always losing it.

An AirTag is a great gift because it feels like a luxury item you might not buy for yourself but would definitely use if someone gave it to you.

6. Spring Dress

Once winter is over, spring will be here, and I always find myself wishing I had a cute new spring dress to wear. I know I probably wouldn’t wear it constantly, but it would still be fun to have.

This is another one of those gifts I would love to receive but probably wouldn’t buy for myself, which makes it a great gift idea. Make sure you tell them which dress you specifically want. There are so many out there, and it’s always better to get exactly what you want than to be disappointed by a dress you can’t or won’t wear.

7. Coupon Book

I’m sure many of us made coupon books for our parents when we were little, but they can also be a fun and thoughtful gift for a significant other.

This is something your partner might actually use. You could include coupons for a home-cooked meal, a romantic date night, doing all the chores, giving a massage, or letting them choose the next movie you watch together.

9. Mop Socks

Mop socks are one of my favorite random gag gifts. Even though they were given to me as a joke, I actually love them!

They’re surprisingly useful whenever I accidentally spill something in the kitchen but have to be out the door in less than a minute. I simply slip one over my shoe and use it to clean up the spill. Then I quickly wet the other one in the sink and use it to remove any leftover stickiness.

Afterward, I toss them in with my load of towels, and they’re ready to use again.

I wear a women’s 9½, and they just barely fit over my shoes. If you wear a larger shoe size, these probably won’t fit over your shoes.

It would give you something peaceful to watch while studying or relaxing.

11. Candle or Scent Plug

There’s nothing quite like making your home smell wonderful! If your dorm doesn’t allow candles, you can get scent plugs instead.

You could also make this gift into a fun experience by going to Bath & Body Works with a friend or loved one. You can take the time to chat, catch up, and find your favorite scents so they know what to get you in the future.

12. Polaroid Camera

I love my Polaroid camera. Even when I don’t use it very often, it makes a cute decoration, and the photos become even more special because they aren’t just pictures sitting on your phone.

They’re physical images that you can put on your wall or keep in your wallet. I also love the vintage look of Polaroid pictures because it makes them feel even more personal.
