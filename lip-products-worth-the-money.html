Lip Products Worth the Money

By Money Well Spent • Retail Beauty Insights • 6 min read

"Lip products can be hit-or-miss, with many leaving your lips dry or fading in minutes. Here are the standalone glosses, primers, lipsticks, and plumpers that actually deliver long-lasting color, real hydration, and serious value."

1. Kylie Cosmetics Lip Gloss

High Shine & Hydration

This gloss lasts a surprisingly long time. The colors are rich and pretty, and I find it less sticky and more hydrating than many inexpensive lip glosses I’ve tried. I especially love the brown shade because it works well for toning down bright lipsticks and giving them a more subtle look. The shine also lasts for a long time.

Pros:
  • Long-lasting shine and color
  • Rich, pretty colors
  • More hydrating than many inexpensive glosses
  • Less sticky texture
  • Brown shade works well for toning down bright lip colors
Cons:
  • I haven’t noticed any major downsides so far
  • Only a couple of colors available on Amazon

2. MAC Lipstick Primer

Lipstick Saver

Do you have a favorite inexpensive lipstick that you just wish would last longer? This primer is a great way to get more wear out of the lipsticks you already own. It helps hydrate your lips while locking in the color you apply over it, making your lipstick last longer. I also like that it can save money in the long run because you can get more wear out of the lipsticks you already have.

Pros:
  • Helps lipstick last longer
  • Hydrates lips
  • Helps lock in lip color
  • Can help you get more use out of inexpensive lipsticks
Cons:

Adds an extra step to your lip routine.

3. MAC Lipstick

Iconic Longevity

If you have a favorite lipstick color that you love but it doesn’t last very long, MAC is worth trying. Their lipsticks last for hours, and they come in a huge variety of colors and finishes. My favorite is the glossy lipstick, but they also offer matte options if you prefer a different finish.

Pros:
  • Long-lasting formula
  • Large variety of colors
  • Multiple finishes available (glossy and matte)
  • Saves money by not needing to reapply frequently compared to drugstore brands
Cons:

Higher price than many drugstore lipsticks.

4. Better Than Sex Lip Plumper

Maximum Plump

This lip plumper definitely lives up to its hilarious name. It is one of the strongest lip plumpers I’ve tried, so this one is definitely for people who want a noticeable effect. Be careful when applying it, though—the formula is meant for your lips and can cause a similar puffing sensation if it gets onto the surrounding skin. I would recommend being careful with how much you apply and keeping it where it belongs.

Pros:
  • Very effective at plumping lips
  • Noticeable, immediate results
  • One of the strongest plumpers I’ve tried
  • Comes in multiple colors
Cons & Precautions:
  • Strong tingling sensation
  • Needs to be applied carefully to avoid getting it on surrounding skin

Important: This product is intended for use on the lips only. Follow the product's directions and discontinue use if you experience an unexpected or severe reaction.

5. Buxom Lip Liner

Built-In Brush

This creamy lip liner is made to last. I’ve had my lip liner pencils for a long time, and the liner itself stays on for hours. Sometimes it even lasts longer than the lipstick I wear over it, which makes it a great option when I want my lip color to stay defined throughout the day.

Pros:
  • Creamy and easy to apply
  • Long-lasting formula that can outlast lipsticks
  • Pencils last a long time
  • No need for sharpening
  • Comes with a blender brush on the end of the pencil
Cons:

May need to be reapplied after eating or drinking.

6. Tarte Maracuja Lip

Ultra-Hydrating

This is one of my favorite lip products to wear. The Maracuja line comes in several different styles, including regular glosses, lightly plumping glosses, and glitter shades. The plumping glosses give a gentle minty feeling and only slightly plump the lips. The glitter shades are also a fun option when I want something a little different.

What I love most about these is how hydrating they are. If you love having color on your lips but find that lipstick or regular lip gloss can be drying, these are worth trying. One downside is that the different versions don’t always come in the same shades, so you may find a similar color rather than an exact match. I’ve tried every variety except for the pH shifting gloss; I’ve posted the link below just in case you’d like to check that out.

Pros:
  • Very hydrating formula
  • Multiple styles and finishes
  • Regular, lightly plumping, and glitter options
  • Great for people who find other lip products drying
  • Fun variety of colors
Cons:

The exact same shades aren't always available across each style.

7. Buxom Lip Gloss Plumper

Shade Variety staple

I love the variety of colors this lip gloss comes in, and it lasts a long time on my lips. There are plenty of shades to choose from, including plumping versions if you want a little extra volume. The only thing I don’t love is that you can’t apply too much at once. If I use too much, it can become stringy when I talk, especially if I’m talking quickly.

Pros:
  • Long-lasting wear
  • Lots of colors to choose from
  • Plumping options available
  • Beautiful high shine
Cons:

Can become stringy if too much is applied at once.
