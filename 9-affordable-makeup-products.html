9 Affordable Makeup Products I Use Every Day

By Money Well Spent • Retail Beauty Insights • 7 min read

"As someone who spent a year working in retail makeup sales, I’ve tested everything from luxury to drugstore. These 9 budget-friendly products are my everyday holy grails that genuinely perform!"

1. Telescopic Mascara

Drugstore Classic

This mascara is phenomenal! My lashes look noticeably longer when I wear it, and I usually apply 2–3 layers to get my desired volume. The mascara stays in place throughout the day without transferring onto my hooded eyelids, which is a huge plus for me. I also love that I can get that long-lasting wear without having to use waterproof mascara, which I find much more difficult to remove. As an added bonus, one tube lasts me a long time!

Pros:
  • Makes lashes look noticeably longer
  • Buildable—I can apply 2–3 layers for more volume
  • Stays in place throughout the day
  • Doesn't transfer onto my hooded eyelids
  • Long-lasting without being waterproof
  • One tube lasts a long time
Cons:

May require multiple layers if you want more volume.

2. Sky High Mascara

Everyday Lengthener

This mascara tube lasts a ridiculously long time! It’s great for lengthening my lashes, and I love how quick and easy it is to apply. The only downside I’ve noticed is that it doesn’t always stay on the very tips of my lashes throughout the entire day, so I usually like to reapply it once during the day. Overall, I really like this mascara when I want something that gives me longer-looking lashes without taking much time to apply.

Pros:
  • Makes lashes look longer
  • Quick and easy to apply
  • One tube lasts a long time
  • Great for everyday use
Cons:
  • Can wear off at the tips of my lashes throughout the day
  • I sometimes need to reapply it once during the day

3. RT Makeup Sponge

Beginner Essential

I’ve had my RT makeup sponge for a long time now, and it’s my favorite one yet! It does a great job of blending my foundation and concealer evenly without making my makeup look splotchy or unnatural. Of course, technique is an important part of using a makeup sponge, but having a good-quality sponge makes a huge difference—especially for beginners who are still learning how to blend their makeup.

Pros:
  • Blends foundation and concealer evenly
  • Helps makeup look smooth and natural
  • Easy to use
  • Great for beginners
  • Holds up well over time
Cons:
  • Technique still plays a role in getting an even finish
  • Like any makeup sponge, it needs to be cleaned regularly

4. NYX 24 Hour Stay Concealer

Holy Grail Drugstore

This is by far one of my favorite drugstore concealers! I’ve been using it for a long time, and I love how long it lasts while helping my skin look smooth and even. One of my favorite things about this concealer is that it creases far less under my eyes than other concealers I’ve tried, which makes a huge difference for me.

Pros:
  • Long-lasting
  • Helps create a smooth, even finish
  • Creases less under my eyes than other concealers I’ve tried
  • Great drugstore option
Cons:

After getting very sweaty or 8 hours of wear, it builds up in my creases a little (not very noticeable but still there).

5. NYX 24 Hour Stay Foundation

Smooth Matte Finish

This has been one of my favorite drugstore foundations for a long time. It lasts throughout the day and gives my skin a smooth, even appearance. I especially love that I can get a great finish and long-lasting wear from a more affordable drugstore foundation.

Pros:
  • Long-lasting
  • Makes my skin look smooth, even, and matte (not shiny)
  • Great for everyday wear
  • Affordable drugstore option
Cons:
  • Stays even longer when a setting powder is applied
  • After getting very sweaty or 8 hours of wear, it builds up in my creases a little (not very noticeable)

Budget Staple

  • Great for switching up your makeup look

7. NYX Slick Click

Pigmented Stain

These pack some serious pigment and apply so easily. The color lasts a long time, even after the initial shine starts to fade. I especially love pairing them with lip gloss because the gloss adds a beautiful shine without messing up the color underneath. It’s an easy way to get a pigmented lip color while still having the option to add extra shine throughout the day.

Pros:
  • Very pigmented
  • Easy and smooth to apply
  • Color lasts even after the shine fades
  • Pairs beautifully with lip gloss
  • Easy to add more shine without disrupting the color underneath
Cons:

The initial shine fades before the color does.

8. e.l.f. Lip Gloss

Non-Sticky Hydration

This lip gloss is so pretty and lasts a long time. If you’re looking for a hydrating gloss, this is definitely one of my favorites. It feels like a lip gloss and lip balm in one, keeping my lips hydrated while still giving them a beautiful shine. It also comes in so many pretty colors, which makes it easy to find a shade that works for you.

One of my favorite things about this gloss is that it isn’t overly sticky or stringy. I hate when lip gloss gets stringy while I’m talking, and I haven’t had that problem with this one. It feels comfortable on my lips while still giving me the glossy finish I want.

Pros:
  • Hydrating and comfortable
  • Gives lips a beautiful shine
  • Doesn't feel overly sticky or stringy
  • Works like a lip gloss and lip balm in one
  • Comes in a variety of pretty colors
  • Lasts a long time
