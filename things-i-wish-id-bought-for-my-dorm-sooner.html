Things I Wish I’d Bought for My Dorm Sooner

Useful storage, cozy details, and everyday essentials that make dorm life easier.

1. 3-Tier Storage Bin

My experience

Extra storage can make a huge difference in a dorm room. The storage space you’re given may not always be enough for everything you brought with you, and an extra three-tier storage bin can help prevent overflowing drawers and cluttered desks.

If your bed is raised, you can slide storage bins underneath it. A three-tier bin can also sit on top of or next to your dresser and essentially give you an extension of your existing storage space. They’re useful for everything from small items and knickknacks to things you would rather keep somewhere more private, such as snacks, personal items, or other belongings you don’t want left in a shared space.

Pros

  • Adds extra storage.
  • Helps reduce clutter.
  • Can fit under raised beds.
  • Can be used next to or on top of a dresser.
  • Useful for keeping personal items organized.
  • Wheels make it easier to move.

Drawbacks

  • Extra storage bins can take up valuable floor space if you don’t have room under your bed or on your dresser.
  • Adding too much weight can break the plastic.
  • Can be difficult to move without wheels.
  • Options with wheels may cost more.

2. Fairy Lights / Vines

My experience

Having fairy lights around your bed can make your dorm feel much more homey while also giving you a convenient way to turn off the lights without getting out of bed. They’re perfect for those nights when you’re already comfortable under the covers and ready to go to sleep. Instead of getting up, you can simply grab the remote and turn them off. They’re an inexpensive way to add a little extra comfort and personality to your bedroom.

Pros

  • Makes the room feel more homey.
  • Adds a cozy atmosphere.
  • Can be turned off from bed with a remote.
  • Affordable.
  • Adds personality to your space.

Drawbacks

  • Setting them up can take quite a bit of time when you first move in.

3. Step Stool

My experience

Dorm beds are often raised to create extra storage space underneath. Even if your bed isn’t raised all the way, you may still want to lift it enough to fit storage bins or a dresser underneath. The downside is that getting into your bed can become quite the challenge.

A small step stool makes getting into a raised bed much easier. While your roommates are attempting to launch themselves onto their beds, you can simply step up and get in without the hassle.

Before you buy: Keep in mind the height of your bed compared to the height of the stool before purchasing to make sure you choose the right size for your dorm.

Pros

  • Makes getting into a raised bed easier.
  • Useful for reaching high storage.
  • Small and easy to store.

Drawbacks

  • A stool can become a tripping hazard in a smaller bedroom. It may be worth sliding it under your bed when you aren’t using it so you don’t accidentally stub your toe.

The first option is nice because it’s adjustable. If you move around, it may be worth investing in since your bed height can change depending on where you live. It also looks nice in general.

4. Drawer Organizers for the Bathroom

My experience

These are seriously such a lifesaver every time I open my bathroom drawer! They keep everything organized and prevent items from sliding around and creating a mess. Having everything in its own spot also helps me get ready faster in the morning.

If you have leftover organizers, you can use them in your desk, bedroom, or even your fridge to organize other things.

Pros

  • Keep items organized and prevent them from sliding around.
  • Make it easier to find what you need while getting ready.
  • Can be used in different spaces around your home.

Drawbacks

  • Require occasional washing, especially when used for toothbrushes, hairbrushes, or food.

5. Medium to Large Bedroom or Bathroom Trash Can

My experience

A good-sized trash can is an absolute necessity for anyone coming to school. Small trash cans fill up so quickly, especially when you share a bathroom with multiple people. If you’re tired of taking the trash out every other day, a medium or large one is the way to go.

I also love having a larger trash can in my bedroom. It’s convenient to have somewhere nearby to throw things away without constantly needing to empty it. It might not be the most exciting purchase, but trust me when I say this is worth the money.

Pros

  • Hold more trash, so they don’t need to be emptied as frequently.
  • Especially useful in shared bathrooms.
  • Convenient to keep in your bedroom for everyday use.

Drawbacks

  • Take up more floor space than smaller trash cans.

6. Bathroom Mat

My experience

A bathroom mat is a must-have for everyone. It’s nice to have somewhere to step after a shower instead of directly onto the bathroom floor. It also helps catch drips so you aren’t leaving puddles behind.

This can also help prevent arguments with roommates over who left the floor wet and dirty after a shower. It’s always good to have one less roommate quarrel, haha.

Pros

  • Provides a comfortable place to step after showering.
  • Helps keep water off the bathroom floor.
  • Useful when sharing a bathroom with roommates.

Drawbacks

  • None experienced.
