Products for Surviving Accutane

Affiliate disclosure: This post contains affiliate links. If you buy through these links, I may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. I share my own experience with products I've used and clearly identify alternatives based on research.

1. Vanicream Moisturizing Cream

My experience

This thick, hydrating moisturizer was my saving grace during Accutane. It helped combat the dryness I was experiencing without making my skin feel worse. I applied it to my face and body every morning and night to help keep my skin moisturized.

Pros

  • Very hydrating, especially for dry skin.
  • Thick consistency that works well for both the face and body.
  • Became an important part of my daily routine during Accutane.

Cons

  • The thick texture may feel too heavy if you prefer lightweight lotions.

2. Tarte Maracuja Juicy Lip Gloss

My experience

Yes, I know this is a more expensive lip product, but it was better for me than any medicated lip balm I tried during Accutane.

Finding lip products with color that also provide enough moisture can be difficult when your lips are extremely dry. I loved how this product felt on my lips, and I found that it lasted a long time. I didn't feel the need to reapply it as often as I did with the medicated lip balms I had been using.

I personally recommend starting with the clear version. If you love it as much as I do, you can try some of the other shades, including the plumping version, which leaves a lovely minty feeling on your lips.

Pros

  • Comfortable and moisturizing on my dry lips.
  • Lasted longer for me than the lip balms I tried.
  • Available in clear, colored, and plumping options.

Cons

  • More expensive than many traditional lip balms.
  • The plumping version may not be everyone's preference.

3. Too Faced Pillow Lip Mask

My experience

If you're not ready to spend money on the Maracuja Juicy Lip Gloss, the Pillow Lip Mask is another option worth considering. These lip masks smell SO good and feel amazing!

Although they're designed to be used as overnight lip masks, I also wore them during the day. However, there's one important thing to know: the shades can show up more strongly on your lips than you might expect from a lip mask.

For example, if you choose an orange shade, applying too much during the day can leave your lips looking orange. Thankfully, shades such as clear and Pineapple Kiss can be more subtle options for daytime wear.

One other thing I noticed is that the color doesn't always apply evenly. If you're planning to wear it to school or work, I'd recommend applying it in front of a mirror rather than putting it on in a hurry.

Pros

  • Lovely scents and a comfortable feel.
  • Can be used overnight or during the day.
  • Some shades are subtle enough for everyday wear.

Cons

  • Some shades can look quite strong when applied heavily.
  • Color may not apply evenly.

4. e.l.f. Lip Gloss

My experience

This hydrating lip gloss was another product I used a lot during Accutane. I love how it feels on my lips, and it adds a pretty finish while helping them feel moisturized.

However, I noticed that it didn't last nearly as long as the Tarte Maracuja Juicy Lip Gloss. When my lips were especially dry, I found myself needing to reapply it more frequently.

I still love this product, especially if you want a more affordable option!

Pros

  • Hydrating and comfortable to wear.
  • Adds a pretty shine.
  • More budget-friendly than the Tarte option.

Cons

  • Didn't last as long on my lips as the Tarte gloss.
  • Required more frequent reapplication during Accutane.

5. COOLA Spray-On Sunscreen

My experience

For all my busy girlies and makeup lovers, this product is worth looking into! I love the convenience of a spray-on sunscreen, especially when I want to reapply sun protection throughout the day without completely redoing my makeup.

COOLA offers spray-on sunscreen options, including a makeup-setting spray with SPF. Just make sure you choose the specific version that fits your needs.

You might wonder why I don't simply recommend sunscreen lotion. During Accutane, I found that some lotions stung my sensitive skin, and I struggled to find options that felt comfortable with how dry my skin had become. A spray was a convenient alternative for me.

Pros

  • Convenient for reapplying sun protection.
  • Offers options designed to work with makeup.
  • Easy to keep in your bag for busy days.

Cons

  • Can be more expensive than traditional sunscreen.
  • Spray application requires care to ensure adequate, even coverage.

6. Neutrogena Sunscreen

My experience

I LOVE this sunscreen! It's lightweight, dries quickly, and is easy to apply. I also appreciate that it doesn't leave a white cast on my skin and is oil-free while still offering a high SPF.

Even after finishing Accutane, I continued using this sunscreen daily to protect my skin. It also works nicely underneath makeup.

Like many sunscreens, it does leave a sheen on my face. I think it gives the skin a glowy look, but I personally prefer a more matte makeup finish. To get the look I want, I simply apply a light layer of powder over it.

Pros

  • Lightweight and quick-drying.
  • Doesn't leave a noticeable white cast on my skin.
  • Works well underneath makeup.
  • Easy to incorporate into a daily routine.

Cons

  • Leaves a sheen that may not suit people who prefer a matte finish.

7. ScarAway Silicone Scar Sheets

My experience

Anyone who has dealt with acne for a long time knows that the marks can stick around long after a breakout has healed. After finishing Accutane, I used ScarAway silicone sheets on some of my acne marks, usually overnight.

The biggest problem I had with these sheets was that I'm a VERY active sleeper, haha! I'd wake up in the morning and find that the sheet had come off my face or back and ended up in my hair or on my pillow.

Thankfully, I found that the sheets were fairly easy to peel out of my hair or remove from my bedding. I also liked that they were reusable. I would wash the sticky side according to the product's care instructions and reuse the sheets for up to about two and a half weeks.

Over time, I noticed that my scars became much less noticeable. I can hardly see some of the marks anymore. I know that natural healing along with these sheets sped up the process.

Pros

  • Reusable, depending on the product's care instructions.
  • Can be worn overnight.
  • Silicone sheets may help improve the appearance of certain raised scars.

Cons

  • Can come loose while sleeping.
  • May be inconvenient if you move around a lot at night.

Final Thoughts

Accutane can be a challenging experience, especially when you're dealing with dry lips, sensitive skin, and lingering acne marks. These are some of the products that helped me manage those challenges during and after treatment.

Everyone's skin is different, so what worked for me may not work the same way for you. I hope sharing my experience helps you narrow down your options and find products that fit your needs and budget.
