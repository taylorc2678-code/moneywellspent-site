Products for Surviving Accutane

Accutane can be rough on your skin and lips. These are the products that helped me manage the dryness and deal with acne marks during and after treatment. I'm sharing what worked for me, plus a couple of sunscreen options I've researched but haven't personally tried.

Affiliate disclosure: This post contains affiliate links. If you buy through these links, I may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. I share my own experience with products I've used and clearly identify alternatives based on research.
01 · MOISTURIZER

Vanicream Moisturizing Cream

This thick, hydrating cream was my saving grace during Accutane. It gave my skin the moisture it needed without making my skin feel worse. I used it on my face and body every morning and night to help battle the dryness I was experiencing.

PROS

  • Very hydrating, especially when your skin feels extra dry.
  • Can be used on both your face and body.
  • A simple product to keep in your daily routine.

CONS

  • The thick texture may feel heavy if you prefer lightweight lotions.

02 · LIP CARE

Tarte Maracuja Juicy Lip Gloss

Yes, I know this one costs more than a regular lip balm, but it was one of the products I reached for most when my lips were extremely dry. I found it more comfortable and longer-lasting for me than the medicated lip balms I had tried. If you're unsure about the colors, I'd start with the clear version and then try a colored shade or the plumping version if you want that minty feeling.

03 · LIP MASK

Too Faced Pillow Lip Mask

I love the scent and feel of this lip mask. I used it overnight and during the day. Just know that some of the shades can show up more strongly than you might expect. For example, the orange shade can look quite orange if you apply too much, while the clear/Pineapple Kiss option is more subtle. Some colors can go on unevenly, so I recommend applying it in front of a mirror.

PROS

  • Comfortable, moisturizing feel.
  • Can be used overnight or during the day.
  • Some shades give a subtle tint.

CONS

  • Some shades are bold and may take a little care to apply evenly.

04 · AFFORDABLE LIP GLOSS

e.l.f. Lip Gloss

This is a good more affordable option if you want something hydrating and pretty. I used it during Accutane, too. When my lips were at their driest, it didn't last as long as the Tarte gloss for me, but it is a nice option if you don't want to spend as much.

PROS

  • More affordable.
  • Hydrating feel and pretty shine.

CONS

  • Did not last as long for me when my lips were extremely dry.

05 · SUN PROTECTION

COOLA Spray Sunscreen

When my skin was sensitive, some lotion sunscreens stung. I liked the convenience of a spray sunscreen, especially on busy days or when I was already wearing makeup. COOLA also has makeup-setting spray options with SPF, which may be appealing if you want to combine steps. Make sure to apply enough sunscreen and reapply as directed.

06 · EVERYDAY SUNSCREEN

Neutrogena Sunscreen

I LOVE this sunscreen. It's lightweight, dries quickly, and is easy to apply. It doesn't leave a white cast on me, and I like the glowy finish it gives my skin. It also works well under makeup for me. If I want a more matte look, I just powder over it.

WHY I LIKE IT

  • Lightweight and quick-drying.
  • No white cast on me.

  • Oil-free, with a high SPF in the version I use.

07 · ACNE MARKS & SCARS

ScarAway Silicone Scar Sheets

After Accutane, I used these silicone scar sheets on acne marks on my face and back, usually overnight. I'm a VERY active sleeper, so the sheets sometimes came off and ended up in my hair or on my pillow. Even so, I found them easy to peel off and reuse according to the care instructions. I washed mine and reused them for up to about two and a half weeks.

Over time, my scars faded and became much less noticeable. I've gotten to a point where I can hardly see my scars anymore. I know that natural healing along with these sheets sped up the process for me.

MY OPTIONS

MULTIPLE SHEETS, SAME SIZE →MULTIPLE SIZES →

Results vary from person to person. Follow the product's instructions for placement, cleaning, reuse, and when not to use the sheets.

FINAL THOUGHTS

What Helped Me Most

Accutane can be challenging, especially when you're dealing with dry lips, sensitive skin, and acne marks that take time to fade. These are the products that helped me manage those challenges during and after treatment. Everyone's skin is different, so what worked for me may not work the same way for you, but I hope this gives you a useful starting point.

If you're currently taking Accutane, follow your prescriber's advice about skincare and sun protection, and ask them about products if you're unsure what is safe for your skin.
