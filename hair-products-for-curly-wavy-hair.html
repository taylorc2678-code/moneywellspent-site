Hair Products I Use for My Curly/Wavy Hair

Finding products that keep my curls soft and help control frizz has taken some trial and error. These are the styling products, shampoos, and conditioners I use for my curly/wavy hair, along with what I love about each one!

1. OUAI Anti-Frizz Styling Cream

My experience

I absolutely love this stuff! I received it for free while working at Ulta so I could try it and recommend it to customers. Let’s just say I got hooked!

The frizz control is phenomenal, and it doesn’t leave my hair feeling particularly crunchy or rough. Once my hair dries, I simply scrunch it, and I don’t feel the need to follow up with hair oil to soften it.

A little also goes a long way, which helps make the higher price feel more worthwhile.

Pros

  • Lightweight.
  • Provides amazing frizz control for my hair.
  • Leaves my hair feeling moisturized.
  • Only requires a small amount per use.

Drawbacks

  • More expensive than the other styling products I use.

2. Garnier Leave-In Conditioner

My experience

I use this after every shower and whenever I’m having an extra-frizzy hair day. I love the way it smells and how soft it leaves my hair feeling.

It helps smooth my frizz and flyaways while giving my hair a fresh scent. It’s become one of those products I consistently reach for in my routine.

Pros

  • Leaves my hair feeling soft.
  • Helps reduce frizz and flyaways.
  • Smells great.
  • Useful after showering or for refreshing frizzy hair.

Drawbacks

  • None experienced.
Shop Garnier Leave-In Conditioner →

3. Garnier Air-Dry Curl Cream

My experience

This is the curl cream I’m currently using. OUAI is still my favorite because of the results I get and how little I need, but this is my second choice, especially since I don’t live near an Ulta right now.

It does a great job of moisturizing my curls and helping them hold their shape. I use a little more of this cream than I do of the OUAI product to get the hold I want throughout the day, but I appreciate the more affordable price and generous tube size.

Pros

  • More affordable than the OUAI cream.
  • Smells great.
  • Helps my curls feel moisturized and hold their shape.
  • Comes in a generous tube that lasts me a while.

Drawbacks

  • I need more product per use than I do with OUAI.
  • The scent may not be for everyone.
Shop Garnier Air-Dry Curl Cream →

4. Garnier Hair Oil

My experience

Whenever I use a curl gel instead of a curl cream, this is what I reach for to soften that crunchy feeling afterward.

I use it to scrunch my dried curls, which leaves them feeling softer and looking shinier and less frizzy. It’s a helpful finishing step when I want the hold of a gel without leaving my hair feeling stiff.

Pros

  • Helps soften the crunchy feeling left by gel.
  • Adds shine.
  • Helps reduce the appearance of frizz.
  • Leaves my curls feeling softer.

Drawbacks

  • None experienced.
Shop Garnier Hair Oil →

5. Garnier Fructis Curl Shine Conditioning Jelly

My experience

This is a great option for my girlies who hate the crunchy feeling some curl creams and gels leave behind! It feels so lightweight that I can barely tell it’s there after my hair dries and I scrunch it.

Another thing I appreciate is that although I notice a scent while applying it, I don’t notice it once my hair is dry.

The trade-off is that I use more product to get the hold I want, so I go through it faster. It also isn’t as hydrating as I’d like.

Pros

  • Lightweight.
  • Doesn’t leave my hair feeling crunchy after scrunching.
  • Affordable.
  • The scent doesn’t linger in my hair once it dries.

Drawbacks

  • I use more product to achieve my preferred hold, so I finish it faster.
  • Doesn’t provide as much hydration as I’d like.
Shop Garnier Curl Shine Conditioning Jelly →

6. Suave Sleek and Shine Shampoo and Conditioner

My experience

This shampoo and conditioner always leave my hair feeling moisturized. My hair can get dry and frizzy, and I love how much softer and shinier it looks after using them.

They also smell amazing! If you can’t already tell, a good scent is something I really appreciate in my hair products, haha.

Pros

  • Leave my hair feeling hydrated.
  • Smell amazing.
  • Add shine.
  • Help smooth my frizz.

Drawbacks

  • None experienced.

7. Aussie Miracle Moist Shampoo and Conditioner

My experience

This set can cost a little more upfront, but you get so much product that it lasts me forever! For how long I can use it, I feel like it’s a great value.

I love the scent, and it leaves my hair feeling hydrated. It’s another set I enjoy using when my hair needs some extra moisture.

Pros

  • Leave my hair feeling hydrated.
  • Smell great.
  • Generous bottles that last me a long time.
  • Good value for the amount of product.

Drawbacks

  • Higher upfront cost.

Final Thoughts

These products each have a place in my routine, whether I’m trying to control frizz, soften my curls, or add some extra shine. OUAI is my favorite styling cream, but I also appreciate having more affordable options that work well for my hair.

Everyone’s curls and waves are different, so you don’t need every product on this list. Hopefully, sharing my experience helps you find something that fits your hair, routine, and budget!
